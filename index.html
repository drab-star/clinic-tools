A complete tooth-by-tooth chart — gums, cavities and restorations in one place. The Contour draws each gum site as a cross-section (teal line = pocket base, shading = the pocket, dashed rules at 3 and 6 mm), while each tooth square records caries and existing dental work. Click a site to enter its numbers, tap a tooth to mark restorations, or tap a surface to flag decay — the Postcard below mirrors the whole mouth, so deep pockets, caries and past work all show up at a glance.

Click a site on the contour to enter pocket, recession and bleeding. Tap the tooth number for status and restorations, or a tooth surface to mark caries.
Contour — depth as a cross-section

How to fill it

  • Click any point on a band to record that site's pocket depth, recession and bleeding; use Prev / Next to move along.
  • Tap a tooth number to set its status and tick restorations (crown, inlay, onlay, fillings) — they fill the tooth square in teal.
  • Tap a surface of the tooth square to mark caries in red (tap again to clear).
  • Or use Use AI to input (Chrome) and just say it — e.g. “5 mm pocket distal of 36”, “distal caries on 36”, or “mark 2 mm pocket in the rest of the teeth”.

How to read it

  • Teal line = pocket base, grey line = gum margin; the shading between them is the pocket.
  • Dashed rules = 3 mm and 6 mm — a line past the lower rule is a deep pocket.
  • Red dots = bleeding; the number above each site is pocket depth in mm. The postcard below mirrors it all.
  • Generate AI report turns the chart into a periodontal assessment; Download PDF saves the chart and the report together.

Your competitor's patients already told you what works and what doesn't — in their reviews. Pull a rival clinic's reviews from Google and Practo (and paste any others), and AI finds the pattern: what patients love, what frustrates them, and the gap you can fill.

How to use it

  1. Paste a competitor's Google Maps link and click Fetch Google reviews — then confirm it's the right clinic to add their reviews.
  2. Optionally add their Practo link the same way, and paste any Justdial or other reviews into the box.
  3. Click Analyse reviews — AI reads them all together and shows what patients love, what frustrates them, and the gap you can fill.

Pulls up to ~80 of the competitor's Google reviews automatically (can take up to a minute).
